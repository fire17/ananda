p2p

Tiny zero-dependency P2P chat framework — one 26-char key is your whole contact surface; E2E-encrypted with a provably MITM-proof first contact (Noise IK over a 110-bit identity commitment), NAT traversal (in-house STUN + ICE-lite punch), and rendezvous over free public infra only (mDNS + BitTorrent Mainline DHT + WSS trackers) with no coordinator server. v0.2.0 adds a zero-build browser client (p2p.akeyo.io/app/, pair + group chat, same protocol source), private one-time invites (IPs AEAD-sealed on all public infra; only the invitee can locate/decrypt — live-proven over DHT + trackers), and sender-key groups. ~5k LOC, Node built-ins only.
