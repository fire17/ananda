Four binary axes of the registry make exactly a hypercube. Every creation sits on one of the 16 vertices; a state change is one edge. Disc area = count. Hue = how far toward "public + shipped" (w). Hover a vertex.

xkind: making (project/tool) vs describing (skill/doc/experiment)
ystatus: in-flight (idea/wip) vs settled (working/shipped/archived)
zvisibility: private vs shareable/public
wpublish: held (undecided/keep-private/ready) vs shipped (published)
